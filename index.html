⚔ Clash Chess

How it works

Pieces move like chess, but a capture is only an attempt. The two pieces duel, trading strikes (attacker first). Stop the marker in the green zone to hit, in the gold centre for double damage. The winner holds the square; the loser leaves the board.

Wounds are permanent. There is no check or checkmate: you win by defeating the enemy king in battle.
